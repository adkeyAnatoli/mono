# Crown Resorts Australia: Explore All Three Properties Today

From the riverside towers of Melbourne to the harbourside skyline of Sydney, Crown Resorts Australia has built three very different destinations under one brand. Each property brings its own mix of hotels, dining, entertainment and gaming, shaped by local geography and the regulatory path it's taken over the years.

## Melbourne: The Southbank Giant

Sitting on the southern bank of the Yarra River, Crown Casino Melbourne is the largest single-site entertainment complex in the Southern Hemisphere, spanning roughly 510,000 square metres in total. It's less a casino with a hotel attached and more a self-contained precinct built for multi-day stays.

### Accommodation and Scale

Three separate hotel towers sit on the one site, giving guests a genuine choice of style and price point without leaving the property.

- **Towers Melbourne**: 481 rooms, generally the top-tier option for guests wanting the fullest experience.

- **Metropol Melbourne**: 658 rooms, the largest of the three towers by room count.

- **Promenade Melbourne**: 465 rooms, a slightly more budget-friendly option within the same complex.

### Conference and Event Spaces

Beyond Crown Casino accommodation, Melbourne carries serious capacity for large-scale functions, conferences and live entertainment.

- **Crown Conference Centre**: 7,350 square metres of event space spread across three floors.

- **Palladium**: a 1,500-seat ballroom used for major banquets and functions.

- **The Palms**: a 900-seat cabaret-style venue for smaller shows and events.

## Perth: The Region's Only Major Resort

On the eastern bank of the Swan River, Crown Casino Perth holds a somewhat unique position, remaining the only large-scale integrated resort anywhere in Western Australia. That lack of local competition shapes much of how the property positions itself.

### Hotel Towers and Room Options

Perth runs the same three-tower structure as Melbourne, though at a smaller overall scale.

- **Towers Perth**: 500 rooms, recognised previously as Best Overall Hotel of the Year by the Australian Hotels Association.

- **Metropol Perth**: 397 rooms, positioned as a mid-tier option within the resort.

- **romenade Perth**: 291 rooms, rounding out the property's accommodation offering.

### Theatre and Entertainment Venues

Crown Casino Perth standout feature on the entertainment side is its dedicated theatre, which regularly hosts touring productions and major concerts.

- **Theatre Perth**: a 2,300-seat venue built for musicals and large-scale performances.

- **Grand Ballroom**: seating for 1,800 guests across banquet and conference functions.

- **Riverside cycling trails**: outdoor paths along the Swan River, a point of difference not found at the other two properties.

## Sydney: The Harbourside Icon

Rising 271.3 metres above Barangaroo, Crown Casino Sydney is housed inside the tallest building in the city, a striking departure from the sprawling, horizontal layout of Melbourne and Perth.

### A Single, Exclusive Tower

Unlike the other two cities, Sydney runs just one hotel rather than three separate towers.

- **Towers Sydney**: around 349 to 350 rooms and suites, plus a separate collection of roughly 76 to 82 private residences higher up in the building.

- **Sky Deck**: a rooftop viewing and event space near the top of the tower.

- **Infinity pool**: marketed as the only one of its kind in Sydney, heated year-round with harbour views.

## Dining and Entertainment Across the Network

Food and shows make up a huge part of what keeps guests on-site for multiple days, and each resort has built its own distinct scene around both.

### Restaurants Worth Booking Ahead For

The spread of Crown Casino restaurants across the network runs from quick bites to genuine fine dining, with some names appearing at multiple properties.

- **Nobu**: found at both Perth and Sydney, a globally recognised name in modern Japanese cuisine.

- **Bistro Guillaume**: a Melbourne staple offering French-inspired fine dining.

- **a'Mare**: Sydney's waterside Italian restaurant, one of the newer additions to the harbourside precinct.

- **Rockpool Bar & Grill**: a Perth favourite known for its steak-focused menu.

### Live Shows and Performance Spaces

Crown Casino shows vary noticeably by city, with Perth leaning hardest into large-scale touring productions.

- **Crown Theatre productions**: major musicals and concerts staged regularly in Perth.

- **Live music and sports screenings**: found across Melbourne's bars and lounges most evenings.

- **Cabaret performances**: hosted at The Palms in Melbourne on a rotating schedule.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which Crown Resorts property has the most rooms?

Melbourne carries the largest room count by a clear margin, with 1,604 rooms spread across its three towers. Perth follows with 1,188 rooms, while Sydney offers a much smaller footprint at around 349 to 350 rooms and suites. The gap largely reflects each city's different approach to scale and exclusivity.

### Can I visit all three resorts on one trip around Australia?

Absolutely, many visitors combine stops in Melbourne, Perth and Sydney given the distinct atmosphere each property offers. Domestic flights connect all three cities regularly, making a multi-stop itinerary fairly straightforward to plan. Loyalty points and rewards also carry across the network, so status earned at one resort benefits stays at another.

### Do all three resorts offer the same dining and entertainment options?

No, each city has developed its own distinct scene, though a handful of Crown Casino restaurants like Nobu appear at more than one location. Perth leans heavily into large-scale theatre productions, while Melbourne focuses more on live music, bars and cabaret-style venues. Sydney takes a quieter, more exclusive approach overall, with fewer dining options but a stronger focus on fine dining and views.
