# Crown Rewards Australia: Join Once, Use It in Three Cities

Crown Rewards Australia is the loyalty program that covers all three resorts in Melbourne, Perth and Sydney. One card earns points on hotel stays, meals, shopping, events and gaming, and those points pay for most of the same things. Sign up before your first visit, as the card starts earning the moment you get it.

## Join and Sign In

Joining takes a few minutes at the resort, and the app handles everything after that. Do both on your first day, before you spend anything.

### Sign Up at an Express Kiosk

* **Find a kiosk:** Express Kiosks stand near the main entrances and the loyalty desks.

* **Scan your ID:** Bring a driver's licence or passport. The kiosk reads it and fills in your details.

* **Take a photo:** The kiosk takes your picture for the card record.

* **Set a PIN:** Choose a PIN you'll remember and keep it to yourself.

### Use the App

* **Sign in:** Use your membership details for Crown Rewards login and check your balance straight away.

* **Plan your visit:** Book rooms at member rates, look up restaurants and track your points in one place.

* **Know the limits:** The app manages your membership and bookings. 

## Earn Points and Status Credits

The Crown Rewards tiers program runs on two currencies: points you spend and status credits that decide your tier. Every dollar you spend earns points, and points in turn convert into credits.

### Points Per Dollar

* **Dining and bars:** 1 point for every A$1 you spend.

* **Hotel stays:** 2 points for every A$1 on your room.

* **Gaming:** Carded play and table play earn casino points on the floor.

* **Caps:** A single corporate event booking earns up to 30,000 points. Event and retail spend earns up to 30,000 a year.

### How Points Turn Into Status

Crown Rewards benefits from different categories convert at different rates.

| Category | Points needed for 1 status credit |
| ----- | ----- |
| **Casino (table games)** | 500 |
| **Hotel stays** | 1,000 |
| **Food, drinks, shopping and events** | 1,500 |

## The Five Tiers

The tiers run from Member to Black, and your status credits decide where you sit. The program reviews every member's tier once a year.

### Tier Ladder

* **Member:** You reach this tier the moment you join. Earn 250 lifestyle points or 25 casino points to unlock 24 hours of free parking in the multi-level car park.

* **Silver:** The first tier above entry level. You move up by collecting status credits across the program year.

* **Gold:** A mid-tier status for regular visitors. Gold members in Sydney get priority access to the gaming floor.

* **Platinum:** The top tier you can earn on your own. Higher tiers add valet parking to the benefit list.

* **Black:** By invitation only. Black members get a personal host who handles bookings and requests.

### How the Crown Rewards Tiers Work 

* **Program year:** It runs from 1 October to 30 September.

* **Tier review:** On 1 October, the program checks your credits and sets your tier for the next year.

* **Thresholds:** The exact credit targets for each tier sit in the program's PDF brochure. Download it before you plan a push for the next level.

## What You Get

Crown Rewards benefits go well beyond the gaming floor. 

### Everyday Benefits

* **Member hotel rates:** Personal prices at all seven hotels, updated daily and available year-round.

* **Pay with points:** In Melbourne and Perth, spend points on meals, shopping, gaming and rooms. Split any bill between points and cash.

* **Free parking:** 24 hours in the multi-level car park once you reach the Member tier threshold.

* **Cinema discounts:** Cheaper movie tickets through Choovie.

### Recent Changes

* **Spa offers:** From 1 October, seasonal spa deals replace the old standing spa discount.

* **Lifestyle focus:** Points and perks now lean towards dining, hotels and events rather than gaming alone.

## One More Tip

Book your room with your card, not through a travel site. Hotel stays earn 2 points per AUD 1, and 1,000 hotel points make one status credit, so AUD 500 on rooms earns the same credit as AUD 1,500 on food and drinks. If you're close to the next tier, move a planned stay into the current program year before 30 September. Check your balance in the app the morning after checkout to make sure the points landed.

## FAQ

### What can I do with Crown Rewards login?

Sign in through the resort app on iOS or Android to check your points balance and track your status credits. From there, book rooms at member rates and plan meals and events before you arrive. The app handles your membership and bookings only and doesn't offer online gaming.

### When does my tier change?

Your tier changes once a year, on 1 October. The program counts the status credits you earned between 1 October and 30 September. Anything you spend after the review date counts towards next year's tier.

### Which Crown Rewards benefits have nothing to do with gaming?

You get personal rates at all seven hotels, updated daily. In Melbourne and Perth, you can pay for meals, shopping and rooms with points or split the bill with cash. You also get cheaper movie tickets through Choovie, seasonal spa offers and free parking once you reach the Member tier threshold.
