## FAQ

### What ID should I bring?

Bring a current photo ID: an Australian driver's licence, a passport or an Australia Post Keypass card. Perth also accepts official digital licences. A photo of a document on your phone won't pass.

### Do sneakers pass the Crown Casino dress code?

The rules require footwear and turn away torn or soiled shoes, but they don't ban sneakers outright. Clean, neat pairs fit smart casual in Melbourne and neat casual in Perth. Staff make the final call at the door, so leave worn gym shoes at the hotel.

### Why can't I take all my winnings in cash in Melbourne?

State law caps cash payouts at AUD 1,000 to fight money laundering. The cashier pays anything above that by bank transfer or cheque. Bring ID so the payout goes through on the spot.

### Does the Crown Casino age limit apply to the whole resort?

No, the 18+ rule covers the gaming floors and bars. Under-18s can visit the hotels, shops and restaurants that don't serve as bars. Carry ID for anyone in your group who looks close to 18\.

### What happens if I arrive on a public holiday?

In Melbourne, the casino entrances close from 4am to 12pm on Christmas Day, Good Friday and ANZAC Day. Perth has partial closures on the same three days. Hotels, restaurants and shops keep their own hours, so plan a meal while you wait.
